# Render tiling patterns

## What it is
Resolves the pattern set by `scn`/`SCN`: a tiling pattern draws the tile and repeats it, and a shading pattern is handed to shading.

## Governing decisions
**None.**

## Design model
Read from the source and the issues cited; no decision record states it. The paint-time alpha rule (#393) is the maintainer's call, written below with its source.

- `scn`/`SCN` resolves the pattern in the current scope and puts the object (`PatternSelection`) in the graphics state. The tile is drawn in the pattern's `/Resources` scope — [resource name scope](../invariant/resource-scope.md).
- **Only path fills and strokes use the pattern** (`try_fill_with_pattern`, `try_stroke_with_pattern`). Glyphs and stencil image masks paint with `fill_color_rgba`, the last numeric components given at `scn` or the colour before (re-verified, `render_glyph`, `render_image_mask`).
- **`/PaintType` is not read**: an uncoloured (type 2) pattern's cell paints with the colours its own content sets, not the colour given at `scn` (re-verified: `PaintType` occurs nowhere in `justpdf-render/src`). `/TilingType` is not read either.
- **The cell is rendered again for every fill or stroke that paints with it**; nothing caches it between paints (inferred, `render_pattern`).
- The tile is drawn while recorded as a running stream, and painting the same pattern again inside the tile paints with the fill color, without the pattern. The tile is drawn without the page's soft mask — [Content stream recursion](../invariant/content-stream-recursion.md). The tile runs through `nested_stream` (#128): an unmatched `q` in it stays in it, and the page's current path does not reach it. The pattern fill or stroke that paints with the tile goes through `draw_mask`, so the page's soft mask applies there (#129; `tile_is_drawn_without_the_page_soft_mask` asserts both halves).
- **The paint's alpha constant and blend mode apply once, when the pattern is painted; a tiling cell runs with those its stream started with** (#393). Shading and tiling pixmaps are painted at `ca` (fill) or `CA` (stroke) and through the paint's blend mode. A tiling cell is drawn with the fill and stroke alpha and blend mode of `StreamStart` — the state at the start of the stream the pattern is painted in: alpha 1 and Normal on the page and inside a transparency group or soft mask `/G` (which reset them), the values current at `Do` inside a plain form, which inherits them. MuPDF does the same (read raw, `source/pdf/pdf-op-run.c`, master: `pdf_show_pattern` runs the cell after `pdf_copy_pattern_gstate` from the gstate recorded at `scn`, the parent stream's, which copies `fill.alpha`, `stroke.alpha` and `blendmode`; `pdf_show_path` paints a pattern inside `fz_begin_group(…, FZ_BLEND_NORMAL, gstate->fill.alpha)` and `pdf_begin_group` with the paint's blend mode). Chosen by the maintainer over keeping the cell at the paint's alpha (#393, 2026-10-26, a judgement made after seeing the MuPDF source and the overlap case; theirs to reverse). From #391 until #393 the cell ran with the paint's `ca` and blend mode and the tile was painted at 1, so a cell's overlapping objects showed through each other and the blend mode was applied twice (measured 2026-10-26: a red square under a blue one in the cell, `ca 0.5`, overlap (128, 64, 191) where (128, 128, 255) is right; under Multiply the overlap was black where blue is right; a tile stroke ignored `CA`). Pinned by `tests/render_tiling_alpha.rs` (and `a_tiling_pattern_fill_paints_with_the_fill_alpha`, `tests/render_shading_alpha.rs`); mutations keeping the paint's alpha or blend mode in the cell, painting a tile fill or stroke at 1, or not recording a plain form's start each fail a named test. A group's or soft mask's recorded start is alpha 1 like the page's, so not recording it there changes no alpha (only its CTM is pinned, #397).
- **The cell goes through the pattern matrix once** (#396). `render_tiling_pattern` draws it with a scale only, at the resolution of `/Matrix` × CTM × page transform, and returns the shader transform from cell pixels back to pattern space and through `/Matrix`; tiny-skia composes the paint's transform (CTM × page) onto it, so the cell goes through each once. Until #396 the cell was drawn through `/Matrix` and at device scale, and painted with an identity shader transform, so it went through the page transform a second time (measured 2026-10-26 on master: a 20 pt cell of a 10 × 5 strip came out twice its size at 144 dpi and at 300 dpi, and a `/Matrix [2 0 0 2 0 0]` tiling had the strip at x = 25; at 72 dpi a cell that divides the page evenly happens to land right, because the page transform there is a flip about the page's centre). Pinned by `tests/render_tiling_placement.rs`; mutations painting with the identity, dropping the cell-to-pattern scale, leaving `/Matrix` out of the shader or drawing it into the cell as well, scaling after `/Matrix`, or taking the resolution without `/Matrix` each fail a named test (the last only `a_scaled_tiling_keeps_sharp_cell_edges`).
- **The cell is one period from the `/BBox` corner, each copy of the box clipped to it** (#399): the pixmap holds pattern space [llx, llx + XStep) × [lly, lly + YStep), and the content is drawn once for every copy of the box, moved 0, −1, … steps, that reaches it (as many as the box is steps wide or high, at most `MAX_TILE_COPIES` = 16 per axis), each clipped to its moved box; the shader transform starts at (llx, lly). A box no larger than the step is one copy; a larger box overlaps its neighbours inside the period; a smaller one leaves the rest of the step empty. MuPDF tiles the same way (read raw, `source/pdf/pdf-op-run.c`, master, `pdf_show_pattern`: `fz_begin_tile_tid(…, pat->bbox, pat->xstep, pat->ystep, ptm, …)`, and without the tile cache a copy per step from `(area - bbox.x0) / xstep`, each at `fz_pre_translate(ptm, x * xstep, y * ystep)`); ISO 32000-2 §8.7.3.1's "shall be used to clip the pattern cell" is inferred, not read raw. A pattern nested in the cell is placed in the outer pattern's space, a recorded start CTM of translate(−llx, −lly), not moved with the copy, as MuPDF sets the parent CTM to `ptm`; with a single copy the two agree, so that choice is unpinned. Until #399 the cell was [0, XStep) × [0, YStep) whatever `/BBox` said (measured 2026-10-26: `/BBox [-5 -5 5 5]` with a red `-5 -5 10 10 re f` left (18, 18) white; a box of 10 × 10 with content filling the step painted red at (15, 5); a mark at x 20..30 of a 30-wide box was lost). Pinned by `tests/render_tiling_bbox.rs`; mutations dropping the box clip, drawing one copy, copying forward instead of back, leaving the corner out of the copy or the shader, or capping at one copy each fail a named test.
- **A pattern's `/Matrix` maps to the default space of the stream it is painted in** (#397), for tiling and shading patterns alike: `StreamStart::ctm` (`RenderInterpreter::stream_start`) is the CTM at the start of the content stream being executed — identity on the page and in a tiling cell, the form matrix × the CTM at `Do` in a form, a transparency group or a soft mask's `/G` — set where each stream sets its CTM and restored by `nested_stream`. A `cm` inside the stream before the paint does not move the pattern; the tiling's shader transform takes the inverse of the current CTM, since tiny-skia composes it back. MuPDF does the same (read raw, `source/pdf/pdf-op-run.c`, master: `scn` records `gstate_num = pr->gparent`, the gstate at the stream's start, and `pdf_show_pattern` uses `fz_concat(pat->matrix, pat_gstate->ctm)`; shading patterns fill with `pr->gstate[gstate->fill.gstate_num].ctm`). ISO 32000-2 §8.7.3.1 says the same (inferred: the text was not read raw). Until #397 both took the CTM current at the fill (measured 2026-10-26: after `0.5 0 0 0.5 0 0 cm` a shading-pattern fill gave (150, 0, 104) at x = 20 where (202, 0, 52) is right). Pinned by `tests/render_pattern_space.rs`; mutations not restoring it after a stream, not setting it for a direct form, a group form, a soft mask form or a tile, taking the current CTM for either pattern type, or leaving the current CTM's inverse out of the tiling's shader transform each fail a named test.
- **Any finite positive step tiles** (#402): `/XStep` and `/YStep` are taken by size (`abs`), and only 0 and non-finite values are rejected, which falls back to the fill colour; the floor is on the cell's device size (1 pixel), since `/Matrix` can scale a step to any size on the page. Until #402 a step below 1 pattern unit was rejected (measured 2026-10-26: steps of 0.5 under `/Matrix [40 0 0 40 0 0]`, a 20 pt cell, painted the page in the fill colour). Pinned by `tests/render_tiling_steps.rs`; the old floor fails `a_step_below_one_unit_is_tiled`, and accepting a step of 0 fails `a_zero_step_is_not_a_pattern` (it painted nothing). NaN cannot be written as a PDF number, so the finiteness check has no test.
- What bounds the input: a cell is at most 2048 × 2048 pixels (`(step × scale).ceil().max(1.0).min(2048.0)`), so a larger cell is drawn at lower resolution and stretched; at most `MAX_TILE_COPIES` = 16 copies of the box per axis run per cell (re-verified, `render_tiling_pattern`). Nothing bounds how often a cell is re-rendered on a page.

## Reference behaviour
- MuPDF `source/pdf/pdf-op-run.c`, master (read raw for #393, #397, #399, 2026-10-26) — `pdf_show_pattern` and `pdf_copy_pattern_gstate` (the cell's alpha and blend mode, the stream's start CTM a pattern is placed in, the per-step copies of the box).
- ISO 32000-2 §8.7.3.1 (inferred, not read raw) — the pattern matrix maps to the default space of the stream, and `/BBox` clips the cell.

## Blast radius
- [Render shading](render-shading.md) — shading patterns go through the same `render_pattern`, `StreamStart::ctm` placement and paint-time `ca`/`CA` rule; a change to either pattern type's placement must keep the two in step.
- [Raster device](raster-device.md) — the cell is handed over as a repeating tiny-skia shader whose transform is computed here; the device composes the paint's transform onto it.
- [Render transparency](render-transparency.md) — groups and soft mask forms set `stream_start`, which decides both where a pattern is placed and which alpha its cell runs with.
- [Glyph rendering](glyph-rendering.md), [Render images](render-images.md) — text and stencil masks ignore the pattern selection.

## Known holes / open
- Uncoloured (`/PaintType 2`) patterns paint their own colours (above; inferred, not measured).
- A cell larger than 2048 device pixels per side is drawn at lower resolution (above).
- The cell size clamp `(step * scale).ceil().max(1.0).min(2048.0)` keeps clippy's `manual_clamp` allowed: `f64::max` maps a NaN product to 1, where `f64::clamp` would pass NaN through and make `Pixmap::new` fail. Since #402 the steps are checked finite before this, so a NaN can only come from the scale (inferred).
